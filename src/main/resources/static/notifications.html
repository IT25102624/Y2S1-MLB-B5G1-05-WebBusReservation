TRAVELER LIFECYCLE

The 4 Stages of Real-Time Trip Updates

Our system triggers automated alerts at critical touchpoints across your expressway journey.

Stage 01 • Booking

Instant E-Ticket Confirmation

The moment seat reservation and payment are confirmed, an automated notification issues your digital E-Ticket, reserved seat number, and QR boarding token.

Verified on Conductor Terminal →
Stage 02 • Baggage

Luggage & Cargo Tracking

Luggage added during booking is automatically registered. You receive a dedicated QR baggage tag notification with live security hold status and cargo bay allocation.

Tracked via Conductor Scanner →
Stage 03 • Boarding

Departure Reminders & Gate Calls

Automated alerts dispatch 45 and 15 minutes prior to trip departure, informing you of platform bay number (e.g. Bastian Mawatha Bay 03) and check-in readiness.

Punctual Terminal Operations →
Stage 04 • In Transit

Highway Advisories & Support

Operations dispatch real-time alerts for expressway traffic conditions, maintenance rerouting, and official replies to passenger customer care inquiries.

Passenger Privacy & Security

Live alerts contain private trip credentials, including booking reference IDs, seat numbers, baggage claim tokens, and direct replies to passenger support queries. Keeping these accessible only to authenticated accounts ensures traveler safety.

Custom Trip Personalization

Registered passengers receive notifications synchronized to their specific bus number, departure date, and boarding terminal. Only the alerts that impact your travel are delivered to your feed and navbar bell.
