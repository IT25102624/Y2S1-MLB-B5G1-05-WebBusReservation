Frequently Asked Questions

How does Luggage Tagging work? ▾
When you book a ticket on CatchBusLK, visit the Luggage Page, select your active booking reference, and specify the number of bags. You'll receive a unique luggage tag number (e.g. LUG-xxxxxx) to present to the conductor when loading baggage into the bus luggage compartment.
How can I check if my bus schedule has changed? ▾
Check the Alerts Page or click the notification bell in the top navigation bar. System alerts automatically trigger when trips are rescheduled or when your luggage status is updated by station conductors.
How do refunds work if my journey is cancelled? ▾
If you purchased optional Refund Protection during seat checkout, you can request a cancellation and refund through your Account Profile. Refunds are credited directly to your registered Sri Lankan bank account within 7 business days.
