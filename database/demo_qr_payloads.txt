CatchBusLK demo QR payloads
============================

Conductor QR payload:
CONDUCTOR-DEMO-QR

Driver QR payload:
DRIVER-DEMO-QR

You can encode each exact text value into a QR code for camera testing.
The login page also accepts a URL such as:
http://localhost:5173/staff/login?role=CONDUCTOR&token=CONDUCTOR-DEMO-QR

Do not use these demo tokens in production.
